The four wallpapers (001.jpg to 004.jpg: a log cabin under the moon, a decorated tree with presents and a snowman,
a village with string lights, and Santa's sleigh crossing the moon over a pine forest) are original illustrations
drawn for CleanYourTV by `make_wallpapers.py` in this folder, from code alone: no photos, no third-party artwork and
no fonts. They are released under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), like the Halloween
theme's haunted house. Run `python3 make_wallpapers.py` (Pillow and NumPy) to draw the same pictures again.

The launcher's Christmas skin icons (the tree, the gift and the snowflake on the pills) are from Microsoft's Fluent
Emoji 3D, MIT licence, Copyright (c) Microsoft Corporation: https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji. The lit
and dark trees are the same picture with the ornaments dimmed; see `launcher/ICONS-LICENSE-FLUENT-EMOJI.txt`.
