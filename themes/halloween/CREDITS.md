The wallpaper, 001.jpg (a haunted house under the moon), is an original illustration drawn for CleanYourTV
and released under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): no photo and no third-party
material. The theme used to add seven night photos from Wikimedia Commons; they were dropped so the theme keeps
only drawn art. Its name and licence are also in `wallpapers/captions.txt`; this theme keeps `wallpaper_captions`
off, so nothing is written over the picture.

The launcher's Halloween icons (the jack-o'-lantern on the network pill, lit and dark, the bat on Inputs and in the
first-Home flight, the ghost on Settings) are from Microsoft's Fluent Emoji 3D, MIT licence, Copyright (c)
Microsoft Corporation: https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji. The lit and dark pumpkins are the same picture
with the carved face recoloured; see `launcher/ICONS-LICENSE-FLUENT-EMOJI.txt`.

`screenshot.jpg` is a mock-up of Home with this theme built from the first wallpaper, the icons and app tiles cut
from `themes/dussehra/screenshot.jpg`, waiting for a photo of a real TV.
